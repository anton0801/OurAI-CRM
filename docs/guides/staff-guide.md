# Staff guide

How everyday work is done in Castlane. You only see what your role allows; if something is missing,
ask your lead or an administrator (Explain Access shows why).

## Getting in (F02)
Open the invitation link, set your display name and password, set up two-factor authentication if
your role requires it and keep your recovery codes. Then you land on **My Work**.
Change your photo, time zone, theme, notifications and quiet hours in **Settings → Personal
Settings**. Quiet hours delay e-mail, never security alerts; the Inbox always updates immediately.

## My Work and Inbox
* **My Work** shows what is yours: tasks due today (in your time zone), upcoming and overdue, items
  you review, followed tasks, shifts, required reading, publications and measurements due.
  Snoozing a personal reminder never moves a deadline.
* **Inbox** collects notifications. Marking a notification read does not complete the task.
* **Ctrl/⌘ K** searches everything you may see.

## Tasks and time (F06)
* Open a task, read the brief and references, press **Start Work**. Blocked tasks explain which
  task they wait for.
* Required checklist items must be ticked before a task can be completed.
* Track time with the timer (one timer at a time; it keeps running on the server if you close the
  browser) or add entries manually. Submit your week for approval from **Time**.

## Producing content (F04, F06)
1. Work on the content item in **Content**: upload versions (files are checked before they become
   available), complete the checklist, then **Submit for Review**.
2. Reviewers comment on the exact version (points on images, timecodes on video). **Changes
   Requested** comes with concrete comments; fix them in a new version and submit again. An
   approval belongs to one version only.
3. **Duplicate as New Draft** reuses a successful item without copying its metrics, approvals,
   payouts or publications (F11).

## Publishing (F07)
From **Calendar** open a scheduled publication, check the approved file and caption, publish it
yourself on the platform (Castlane never posts for you), then **Mark Published** with the real URL
and time. Castlane then creates the measurement checkpoints (e.g. 24 h and 7 d). If publishing
failed or was cancelled, record it with a reason. Nothing becomes Published just because the time
passed.

## Statistics (F09)
**Metrics** lists expected measurements. Add values with the time you actually observed them, the
period, source and evidence (e.g. a screenshot). Leave unknown values empty — empty is not zero.
Corrections create a revision instead of overwriting. For many values use Import Center (CSV) and
choose Skip or Create Revision for conflicts.

## OFM shifts (F08)
1. **My Shifts** → open your shift and read the previous handover.
2. **Start Shift** (the timer runs on the server), work the operations queue and contacts, register
   results by source, **Pause/Resume** for breaks.
3. **End Shift**, then write the report. Before submitting, hand over open questions or confirm
   **No Open Items**. Your supervisor approves or requests changes.
Private contact notes are visible only to people with contact access and never appear in
notifications or search. Registered sales are verified by Finance separately.

## Library and knowledge
Upload files in **Library** (they stay private; restricted files need extra access). Read required
articles in **Knowledge** and press **Acknowledge** — opening an article alone does not count.

## Finance (finance roles)
Enter income and expenses as drafts with evidence, submit them for posting, record settlements when
money actually arrives or leaves, and run compensation. **Record Payment** records a payment that
already happened; it does not transfer money. Posted entries are corrected by reversal, never
edited.

## When something goes wrong
* **Offline** banner: your changes are not saved yet; retry when the connection returns.
* **Updated by another member**: compare the changes; your input is kept.
* Error messages for server problems include a **Request ID** — send it to an administrator.
